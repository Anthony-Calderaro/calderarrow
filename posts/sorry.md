# How to Say Sorry

Essay idea: A proper apology
restore trust; continue relationship; past, present, future
Similar to Catholic repentence. Unsure if that's because of bias from prior experience in the church, or a happy coincidence

3 Components. Missing one is catastrophic

- What was wrong
- How did it hurt?
- How are you going to prevent it in the future

The who is implied by the person you're apologizing to.

The third step is imporatnt. It needs to be reasonable so that it's possible to achieve, but not so small that it's too easy. It also needs to be believable.

The onlyperson who can decide that is the one you're apologizing to. That's the kicker: You don't determine if the apology was a success. They do.

The flip side is that if you do a genuine, 3 pillar apology and it's rejeced, you have a free conscious. people make mistakes,and as long as the damage is repaired and accomodations are made to prevent a repeat of the mistake, An apology like this should be accepted. Some things are inexcuseable, but that's a matter with number 3. If there's no reasonable thing you can do to prevent it in thefuture, your apology is doomed, it's not their fault.
