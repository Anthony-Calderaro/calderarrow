A home will be a good investment as long as the alternative is so bad
Renting is an expense paid indefinitely. A mortgage is an expense paid for 30 years. Without anything else, this makes a mortgage a better option.

But then we add on the fact that fixed-rate mortgages are protected against inflation, whereas rents can always go up unless regulated. 

And then we add on the fact that when you decide to leave your appartment, you don't get anything. When you leave your home, you receive the proceeds from the sale. Even if you lost 99% of the value of your home, the fact that you get anything at all makes it such a good investment. And while land can become impaired, it never depreciates, so there will almost always be a floor on your property's value.
